Official Match Scorecard Audit Protocol

Scores displayed under “Verified Results” are finalized only after the head referee and both team captains submit and sign the physical match report sheet. If a match is abandoned, disputed, or postponed due to inclement weather or pitch conditions, it is recorded with official notes rather than speculative outcomes.

Protests regarding player eligibility or disciplinary decisions must be submitted through official club representatives to the organizing committee within 48 hours of match completion.
