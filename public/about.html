CONSTITUENCY PROFILE & MANDATE

Mzimba Luwerezi Constituency

An overview of our geographical area, demographic priorities, public governance framework, and the parliamentary mandate of Hon. Fischer Nkhwazi.

Geographic & Community Context

Mzimba Luwerezi is situated in the southern quadrant of Mzimba District within the Northern Region of Malawi. The constituency is defined by its vibrant rural communities, rich agricultural landscapes, and the vital Luwerezi river system which anchors the livelihood and transport corridors of our people.

The community relies predominantly on smallholder agriculture—growing maize, tobacco, legumes, and raising livestock—supplemented by vibrant local trading centers. Reliable road linkages, clean borehole water distribution, educational facilities, and accessible rural healthcare centers form the backbone of local development demands.

Constituency Motto

“Muumoza Kutukula Chigawa Chithu”

Meaning “Together Developing Our Constituency”, our motto embodies an inclusive principle: sustainable progress occurs only when residents, community leaders, and elected representatives plan and evaluate public investments collaboratively.

Vision & Development Mission

Our strategic vision is to build an equitable, resilient constituency where public resources are managed with verifiable honesty, essential public services reach every village, and youth talent is nurtured through sports and vocational empowerment.

1. Essential Infrastructure

Expanding solar-powered and hand-pump boreholes, constructing sturdy school classroom blocks, improving clinic maternity wings, and reinforcing vital river crossings and feeder roads.

2. Radical Transparency

Public disclosure of Constituency Development Fund (CDF) allocations, contractor timelines, and verified physical status. Official assertions must be matched by independent field inspections.

3. Youth & Sports Development

Running an organized, professionally tracked constituency football and netball league across all zones to provide productive engagement, physical health, and talent development.

4. Citizen Direct Access

Empowering every resident to submit verifiable reports on broken amenities, track action on their concerns, and access a strictly confidential pathway for vulnerable safeguarding issues.

MEMBER OF PARLIAMENT

Hon. Fischer Nkhwazi

Parliamentary Representative • Mzimba Luwerezi

Hon. Fischer Nkhwazi represents the people of Mzimba Luwerezi in the National Assembly of Malawi. His legislative and constituency agenda is centered on tangible developmental results: ensuring that public funds allocated through the Constituency Development Fund (CDF) and partner development programmes are spent transparently, with verifiable outcomes on the ground.

Recognizing that development cannot succeed without citizen oversight, Hon. Nkhwazi commissioned this digital public service portal to ensure that every resident can inspect project accounts, report damaged infrastructure, and hold the parliamentary office to the highest standard of public accountability.
