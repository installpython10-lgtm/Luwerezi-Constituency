CIVIC INTEGRITY & SOCIAL AUDITING

Independent Verification Framework

In Mzimba South Luwerezi, no public infrastructure project is certified as completed simply because an administrative claim is filed. Every project must undergo independent ground inspection by community verification committees.

01

Dual-Source Reporting

Official progress statements from contractors and local council desks are stored separately from physical inspection findings logged by community observers.

02

Photographic & GPS Evidence

Certification requires dated on-site photographs, verifiable GPS benchmarks, and interviews with local community beneficiaries (e.g., school committees, water users).

03

Responsible Disclosure

When paperwork is incomplete, the project is marked "Unable to Confirm". This serves as an evidentiary disclaimer, not an unsubstantiated accusation of misconduct.

Standardized Project Status Definitions

Understanding what each status indicator on our public register guarantees to the residents of Mzimba South Luwerezi.

Status Label Official Meaning Verification Condition Required
Proposed Project identified during constituency planning or Area Development Committee (ADC) deliberations. Awaiting budgetary approval and formal funding appropriation.
Approved Funding allocated through CDF, District Council, or partner donor programme; procurement scheduled. Disbursement voucher or council resolution verified.
In Progress Contractor appointed or community procurement underway; active ground works initiated. Interim inspection report confirms works on site.
Delayed Work has exceeded target timeline or halted due to materials, weather, or contractor issues. Documented explanation logged with revised completion target.
Reported Completed The implementing agency or contractor has declared works finished. Pending independent committee ground audit and resident sign-off.
Independently Verified Field inspection completed. Water flowing, classroom in use, or bridge safely open to traffic. Photographic proof, community sign-off, and inspection date verified.
Unable to Confirm Incomplete project documentation or conflicting field reports pending re-inspection. Missing documents clearly highlighted without premature conclusions.
